# Elite Custom Wrap — Website Overview

## What the site is

- A premium website for **Elite Custom Wrap**, a car-wrapping business
- Designed in the brand colors: **piano black** and **bone white**
- Built so customers can see a new wrap on a real car before visiting the shop

## The main attraction: live 3D wrap configurator

- Appears first, directly under the header
- Shows the car in a virtual **wrapping studio** with white walls, strip lights and a polished floor
- Uses **21 detailed 3D models of real cars from 15 brands**
- Lists luxury brands first, followed by everyday brands:
  - Rolls-Royce, Ferrari, Lamborghini, McLaren, Aston Martin, Porsche
  - BMW, Audi, Tesla, Toyota, Nissan, Chevrolet, Ford, Honda, Volkswagen
- Lets customers find a car by brand, from a model dropdown, or by search

## What customers can customize

- Color six areas separately:
  - Body
  - Hood
  - Roof / rear deck
  - Mirrors
  - Wheels
  - Accents
- Pick from **14 curated wrap colors**, including Bone White, Piano Black, Racing Green and Midnight Blue
- Choose any custom color with the color picker
- Switch between **gloss, satin and matte** finishes
- Apply one color to all main painted areas at once
- Tap part of the car to select that area
- Disables areas a particular car model cannot customize, so no button is misleading
- Start from ready-made style palettes, such as monochrome, stealth or racing green

## Viewing the car

- Drag to rotate the car 360°
- Turn automatic rotation on or off
- Jump to front three-quarter, side, front and rear views
- Reset the camera with one click

## Saving and sharing

- Saves the design automatically on the visitor's device
- Creates a share link that reopens the same car, colors and finish
- Downloads a PNG image of the design
- Downloads a consultation brief with:
  - Customer name and email
  - Customer's vehicle
  - Chosen design, colors and finish
  - Project notes
  - A link to reopen the design

## Business content

- Explains the business's services
- Shows the wrap process step by step
- Shows color palette inspiration
- Answers customer questions in an FAQ
- Links to the business's Google profile so customers can contact it or leave a review
- Gives clear calls to action, such as **"Let's create"** and **"Start your project"**

## Languages

- Available in **6 languages**:
  - English
  - Romanian
  - German
  - Spanish
  - Italian
  - Hungarian (Magyar)
- Opens automatically in the visitor's browser language
- Uses English when the browser language is not supported
- Lets visitors change language from the globe icon in the header
- Remembers the chosen language
- Changes language without resetting the car or design
- Keeps text boxes the same size when the language changes

## Mobile and responsive design

- Built **mobile-first**, because most customers will use a phone
- Works on phones, tablets, laptops and large desktop screens
- Uses touch-friendly buttons with a minimum size of 44px
- Includes a mobile menu
- Avoids horizontal scrolling on small screens

## Quality, accessibility and performance

- Supports keyboard navigation and has a skip-to-content link
- Gives screen readers labels for buttons, controls and the 3D preview
- Shows clear focus outlines for keyboard users
- Downloads each 3D car only when the visitor selects it
- Shows progress while a car is loading
- Uses compressed 3D models for faster loading
- Pauses 3D rendering when the preview is off-screen or the browser tab is hidden
- Shows friendly messages when something cannot be loaded or saved
- Can be built as a production-ready version of the site

## Legal and responsible use

- Uses 3D models released under the **CC BY 4.0 license**
- Credits every model's author, source and license on the site
- Excludes models whose licenses restrict commercial use or require derivative works to use the same license
- Labels colors and panel areas as **visual previews**, not exact final results

## Current limitations

- The consultation form downloads a brief but does not send it to the business yet
- Panel areas are approximate on some car models
- Screen colors can look different from real vinyl, so physical samples remain essential
- The catalog includes 21 cars, not every brand or model
